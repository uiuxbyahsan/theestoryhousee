# The Story House — Agent Guidelines & Project Rules

## 1. Brand Voice & Visual Identity
- **Tagline:** *"Stories to read. Journeys to remember. Scents to keep."*
- **Positioning:** The Story House turns memories into something you can hold, read, and smell again — custom photobooks paired with a signature scent created for that story.
- **Visual Aesthetic:** Warm neutrals (cream, linen, parchment `#FAF7F2`), ink/burgundy (`#3B141C`), warm amber/gold accents (`#C68B59`, `#D4AF37`). Evokes candle wax, perfume glass, and aged paper. **Never revert to cool blues, pastels, or generic SaaS aesthetics.**
- **Typography:** Literary serif headlines (Cormorant Garamond / Cinzel) + clean, warm sans-serif for body text (Plus Jakarta Sans).
- **Tone:** Sensory, second-person, gently poetic. Reference smell, touch, and human memory.

## 2. Product Architecture & Fixed Scent Pairing
- **Hero Bundle:** The Story + Scent (599 AED) — default pre-selected on all bundle selectors.
- **Fixed Pairings:**
  - ✈️ Travel → *Sirocco & Salted Fig*
  - 💍 Wedding → *Silk Veil & White Oud*
  - 👶 Baby → *Linen Cloud & Warm Almond*
  - 🤝 Friendship → *Golden Hour & Wild Citrus*
  - 🕊️ Memorial → *Parchment & Frankincense* (Handle with care: muted styling, gentle language)
- **Minimum 40 Photos:** Hard requirement for rich 20-page heirloom spreads.
- **Extra Pages:** +18 AED / page beyond 20 pages.

## 3. Order Flow (WhatsApp First)
- **No cart, no checkout, no payment gateway in v1.**
- Primary conversions happen via dynamic `wa.me` deep links with structured prefilled order details.
